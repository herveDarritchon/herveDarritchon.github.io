Hugo

Guides pratiques pour construire et maintenir un site avec Hugo. Focus sur conventions d’arborescence et workflows.

À l’intérieur :

  • organisation recommandée (leaf bundles, index.md vs _index.md).
  • gestion des ressources (assets/ vs static/), shortcodes et images.
  • archetypes pour standardiser le front matter, et stratégie i18n (suffixes vs contentDirs).
  • commandes courantes : hugo server -D, travail avec thèmes et submodules.

Destiné aux développeurs qui publient du contenu technique avec Hugo et cherchent une structure robuste.

Which platform to blog

Quickly after deciding to Blog, I needed to choose my platform. I have found quite a lot of blogging platforms free on Internet (blogger.com, medium.com, …). But I didn’t want to be locked down to one platform and as a developper I wanted to have the same process as coding. So I shorty felt the need to have something simple, something very similar to my day to day job, it’s why I chose to do my content in markdown and to deploy it as I push code to a git repo.
